# Launch Campaign Planner

Campaigns succeed on focus: one audience, one promise, repeated clearly across channels.

## Steps

1. Define the launch objective in one measurable sentence ("500 trial signups in 3 weeks") and the audience segment that matters most. A launch aimed at everyone converts no one.
2. Write the core message: what changes for the customer, in their words. Derive 2-3 supporting proof points (feature, number, testimonial).
3. Map the audience's existing attention: which channels they already use (email list, communities, social, partners, events). Prioritize owned channels first, then earned, then paid.
4. Sequence the campaign in phases: teaser/preview (build the list, warm the audience), launch day (simultaneous push across chosen channels), follow-up week 1-2 (objection handling, proof content, second wave).
5. Build the channel plan as a table: channel, asset, owner, date, expected contribution. Every asset names its single CTA.
6. Prepare the assets list with specs: announcement email, landing page section, social variants (per platform format), and a press/partner note if relevant.
7. Define measurement: primary metric with baseline, leading indicators (email CTR, page conversion), and a daily check during launch week; decide in advance what slowdown triggers a plan B.
8. Debrief template ready: planned vs actual per channel, lessons, what to reuse.

## Checks

- One primary metric with a baseline and a deadline.
- Every asset has an owner and a date.
