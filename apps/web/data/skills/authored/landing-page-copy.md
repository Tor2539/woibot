# Landing Page Copywriter

Every section answers the question a skeptical visitor is silently asking. Answer in order, or they bounce.

## Steps

1. Fix one conversion goal and one primary audience for the page. Two goals on one page means zero.
2. Hero section answers three questions in five seconds: what is it, who is it for, why should I care. Headline = the outcome in the customer's words; subhead = mechanism or proof; CTA button = the action as a verb with value ("Start free", not "Submit").
3. Below hero, prove it: 2-3 concrete benefits with numbers or specifics, a testimonial with a name and context (no "great product!"), and logos or usage counts if real.
4. Handle objections in the order they arise: price (justify with value math or comparison), effort ("setup in 10 minutes"), trust (guarantee, security, support). Each objection gets its own short section.
5. Explain the mechanism briefly — how it works in 3 steps reduces fear of complexity and qualifies the right buyers.
6. Repeat the CTA after every major section; make the primary path visually continuous.
7. Write the FAQ from real questions (sales emails, support tickets), not imagined ones — 4-6 items max.
8. Tighten: cut every sentence that does not add information, proof, or emotion. Then read the page as a stranger: could you say what this is and what to do next in 10 seconds?

## Checks

- Headline, testimonial, and CTA contain specifics, not adjectives.
- Objections appear before the second CTA.
