# Social Content Calendar Builder

Consistency beats brilliance. A sustainable rhythm of useful posts outperforms sporadic virality attempts.

## Steps

1. Define the audience and the account's job: entertain, teach, sell, or recruit — usually one primary. State the conversion goal (follows, DMs, signups).
2. Set 3-4 content pillars that serve that job (e.g. for a bakery: behind-the-scenes, how-to tips, customer stories, offers). Pillars keep the mix intentional instead of random.
3. Choose the platform mix the team can actually sustain; each platform gets a native format plan (short video, photo carousel, text post) — never paste one format everywhere.
4. Pick the posting rhythm from real capacity: posts per week per platform that survive a busy week. A calendar that dies in week two is worthless; start under capacity.
5. Lay out 2-4 weeks in a table: date, platform, pillar, format, hook (first line), CTA, asset needed, owner, status.
6. Write hooks that pass the thumb test: open with the payoff, a question, or a specific number; no generic greetings. Draft the hook column before writing full captions.
7. Plan the measurement loop: weekly 15-minute review of top/bottom posts by engagement and goal actions; feed the winners into next month's pillars.
8. Include a reuse map: each substantial piece (video, long post) is sliced into 2-3 derivative posts — the calendar should produce more from less.

## Checks

- Every slot has pillar, hook, CTA, owner.
- Rhythm matches real team capacity.
